Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: 18 September 2026

1. Free Shipping Threshold

MDMA Spices and Foods offers Free Standard Delivery on all orders above ₹499 across India. Orders below ₹499 incur a nominal shipping fee of ₹60.

2. Processing & Dispatch Timeline

All orders are processed and freshly packed at our distribution center within 24 to 48 business hours (excluding Sundays and national holidays).

3. Estimated Transit Times

  • Metro Cities: 2 to 4 business days
  • Tier 2 / Tier 3 Cities: 3 to 6 business days
  • Remote & North East Regions: 5 to 8 business days

4. Real-Time Order Tracking

Once dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email notification with your active courier tracking link. You can also track your shipment live on our Order Tracking Page.
