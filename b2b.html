Frequently Asked

B2B & Wholesale Inquiries FAQ

Common questions regarding minimum order quantities, private labeling, and sample dispatches.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for wholesale pricing?

Our standard MOQ for wholesale pricing starts at 50kg per order (which can be a combination of different spices, dehydrated foods, or oils). For initial trial batches and recipe testing, smaller trial quantities can be arranged by contacting our sales desk.

Do you provide physical quality samples before we place a bulk order?

Yes, absolutely! We provide curated sample kits of our Dehydrated Fruitss, single-origin spices, pure oils, and masala blends for kitchen and lab quality testing. Simply check the "Request Product Quality Testing Samples" box on the form above or message our team directly.

Can MDMA customize spice blend formulations and grind mesh sizes?

Yes. We have proprietary in-house slow-grinding and blending facilities. We can formulate customized curry powders, garam masala profiles, chai aromatics, and adjust the particle mesh size (from coarse cracked to ultra-fine) to suit your commercial kitchen or industrial food processing equipment.

Do you offer Private Label (White Label / OEM) manufacturing?

Yes! We provide comprehensive private-label OEM manufacturing for organic retail chains, gourmet food brands, and export distributors. We can package your spices and Dehydrated Fruits crisps in your custom-branded pouches, glass jars, metal tins, or bulk vacuum cartons with complete barcode and nutritional labeling compliance.

What are your commercial payment and delivery terms?

For initial orders, payments are processed via RTGS / NEFT / IMPS / Corporate Bank Transfer against proforma invoice. For established corporate buyers and regular HoReCa clients, customized 15-day or 30-day credit cycles and annual rate-lock contracts are available subject to onboarding verification.
