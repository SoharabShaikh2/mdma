1. Information We Collect

When you visit MDMA Spices and Foods (mdmaspices.com) or make a purchase, we collect necessary personal details such as your name, billing address, shipping address, payment information, email address, and phone number to fulfill orders safely.

We use order information to fulfill all transactions placed through the site, arrange shipping, provide order tracking invoices, and communicate order confirmations and security alerts.
