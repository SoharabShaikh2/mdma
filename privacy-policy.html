Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit MDMA Spices and Foods (mdmaspices.com) or make a purchase, we collect necessary personal details such as your name, billing address, shipping address, payment information, email address, and phone number to fulfill orders safely.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use order information to fulfill all transactions placed through the site, arrange shipping, provide order tracking invoices, and communicate order confirmations and security alerts.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption to protect your sensitive personal data during transmission. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third-party marketing brokers.

4. Contact Us

For privacy queries, contact our Data Officer at privacy@mdmaspices.com or our headquarters in New Delhi.
