Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our spice sourcing, purity testing, packaging, shipping timelines, and storage advice.

What makes MDMA Spices and Foods different from regular market brands?

MDMA Spices focuses on single-origin sourcing directly from heritage spice belts (like Salem for turmeric, Malabar for pepper, and Idukki for cardamom). We employ slow, low-temperature cold grinding to preserve natural essential oils, bioactive compounds (such as curcumin and piperine), and vibrant aromas without adding any artificial colors, fillers, or starches.

Are any artificial preservatives, food colorings, or MSG added?

Absolutely not. Every single spice powder, whole seed, and masala blend from MDMA is 100% pure and unadulterated. We never use synthetic dyes (like Sudan or Metanil yellow), anti-caking agents, MSG, or artificial flavor boosters.

How should I store my MDMA spices to maintain peak freshness?

Keep your spices in their airtight resealable aroma-barrier pouches, or transfer them to dry glass/ceramic jars. Store them in a cool, dark kitchen cabinet away from direct heat, sunlight, and cooking steam. Always use a dry spoon to avoid introducing moisture.

What are your delivery timelines and shipping charges?

We offer Free Standard Delivery on all orders above ₹499. For orders below ₹499, a flat delivery fee of ₹60 is applied. Most orders are dispatched within 24–48 business hours and delivered within 3 to 5 business days across India.

What payment methods are supported?

We support all major Indian payment options including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

What is your return and refund policy?

If your package arrives damaged, unsealed, or incorrect, please notify our customer care team within 7 days of delivery with photos of the package. We will promptly issue a full replacement or refund.

Do you accept bulk, corporate gifting, or restaurant orders?

Yes! We supply boutique restaurants, artisanal kitchens, and corporate gift hampers. Please email us at wholesale@mdmaspices.com or submit our contact form for tailored pricing and packaging options.
